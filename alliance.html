How to use this manual

Browse by topic, search the library for a specific question, or use a play-style path to compare priorities. Each guide brings together the mechanics, trade-offs, worked examples and quick checks for that subject.

Useful knowledge needs context

Strategies depend on your universe, account, class, available time, and risk tolerance. Examples are not a mandatory build order. Historical Orion research is deliberately kept in its own archive, with later changes and validation gaps visible.

Private by design

This static website has no account login, analytics, message sender, or game connection. Optional reading progress is stored only in your browser. Copying or downloading a guide does not send it anywhere automatically.
