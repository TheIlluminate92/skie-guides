A–Z / QUICK REFERENCE

Know the terms.
Follow the plan.

A quick companion to the guides, not a substitute for your universe’s current rules.

ACS
Alliance Combat System: the feature for coordinating eligible fleets in attacks or defenses. Availability and limits depend on the universe and mission.
Anti-Ballistic Missile (ABM)
A silo-stored interceptor for incoming Interplanetary Missiles. It does not shoot down an ordinary attacking fleet.
Artefacts
Lifeform discovery rewards used to select Lifeform technologies deliberately rather than relying on random selection.
Astrophysics
Research connected to colony and expedition capacity. Read its current technology details for your next unlock.
Cargo
The resources a fleet can carry. Fuel and modifiers affect the usable space shown during dispatch.
Class
Your chosen account role, such as Collector, General, or Discoverer. Class bonuses change which activities are attractive.
Debris field
Material left in space after eligible combat, collectible by the appropriate ships. Universe settings affect what becomes debris.
Economy speed
A universe setting affecting economic activity. It is not interchangeable with war or peaceful fleet speed.
Expedition
A mission to position 16 with uncertain outcomes. Distinct from Lifeform discovery missions.
Fields
Planetary building space. Check the remaining capacity before planning a mature colony.
Fleet slot
Capacity to operate another fleet mission. Expedition limits and total fleet limits are separate constraints.
Fleetsave
Sending valuable ships and resources on a carefully planned mission while you are away, timed for a return you can cover.
Holding time
Time a fleet spends at its expedition or holding destination, separate from outward and return travel.
IAS
Interstellar Anomaly Scanner, an Orion building. The historical calculator here models beta13 prices only.
Interplanetary Missile (IPM)
A silo-launched weapon against defense installations. Its targeting, range and interception rules are separate from ordinary fleet combat.
Jump Gate
A moon facility that moves ships between eligible owned moons. It is not a resource transporter.
Lifeforms
Planet-based species, buildings, population, food, and technologies. Species choice and research selection are separate decisions.
Lunar Base
A moon facility that opens building space. Keep enough free space to develop it rather than filling every available field.
Missile Silo
The planetary facility that unlocks and stores anti-ballistic and interplanetary missiles. Capacity is a stock-management constraint, not an automatic defense score.
Nanite Factory
A planetary construction-speed facility. It reduces build time but does not pay the resource cost of a build or directly speed ordinary research.
Payback
A planning estimate: an investment’s cost divided by the extra production it creates. It excludes benefits and risks not counted in the model.
Phalanx
Sensor Phalanx, a moon facility relevant to tracking eligible fleet movements. Fleetsaving choices must account for visibility.
PTS
Public Test Server. Mechanics and balancing seen here are not automatically the rules of a live universe.
Research prerequisite
A required building or technology level that must be satisfied before another unlock becomes available.
Resource bottleneck
The resource currently preventing your next useful action, even when other resource balances look healthy.
Tier
A group of Lifeform research slots with population requirements and technology selections.
Universe settings
The server-specific rules, including economy, research, fleet speeds, debris and fuel settings. Always compare like with like.

For mechanics, prerequisites and source links, use the relevant guide in the chapter menu.
