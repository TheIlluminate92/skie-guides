Fleetsaving
Skie Field Manual · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026. Read the linked reference for formulas, sources and version limits; current account settings remain the final check.

This guide covers:
- Understand why a flying fleet still needs a safe return.
- Distinguish deployment from transport.
- Verify every launch instead of relying on habit.

Protect the return, not only the departure

A flying fleet still needs a protected landing. Choose a time you can cover, allow for reconnecting and reading events, and use the current dispatch display. Defense does not make an unattended valuable fleet safe.

Choose a method you understand

Outbound Deployment is visible at its destination planet, not its origin; recalled Deployment is absent from phalanx. Moon destinations avoid a directly scannable endpoint, but moon destruction and prior observation still matter. Transport has different return visibility.

Details and worked examples
https://theilluminate92.github.io/skie-guides/guide-fleetsave-reference.html#route

Run the departure checklist

Check ships, destination type, mission, speed, cargo, fuel and arrival before sending. Then verify the movement in the event list. Outstanding returns and new shipyard completions can leave valuable ships behind after an otherwise correct departure.

Details and worked examples
https://theilluminate92.github.io/skie-guides/guide-fleetsave-reference.html#launch

Plan around real life

Match the route to your real availability and connection reliability. A narrow alarm window is not a safety margin. Vacation mode has conditions and should not be assumed to fix an already developing attack.

Details and worked examples
https://theilluminate92.github.io/skie-guides/guide-fleetsave-reference.html#timing

Recall timing and threat response

Understand recall timing with a small fleet before relying on it. When threatened, read the actual mission and timing before changing the route. A recall or moon loss can change the return plan; obtain private, situation-specific help rather than publishing live fleet details.

Quick checks:
- The mission and planet/moon types are correct.
- Cargo and fuel fit.
- The event list confirms the launch.
- I can be online before arrival.
- Outstanding returns and queued ships are accounted for.
