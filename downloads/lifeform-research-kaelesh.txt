Kaelesh Lifeform research reference.
Skie Field Manual · Reviewed 9 November 2026. Source-model reference, not a live-account measurement. Official workbook vintage and current implementation caveats are stated below; live Techinfo takes precedence.

This guide covers:
- Find the target and base effect of each technology.
- Separate local research levels from account-wide contributions.
- Check caps, conditions and source-version limits before investing.

How to read this reference

All 18 Kaelesh technologies are listed below. Values are base effects per research level before species experience and applicable building amplification. Each effect contributes across the account only to its named target; selection and research levels remain local to each planet.

Ship-stat package = structural integrity, shields, attack, cargo capacity and base speed, not fuel consumption. Civilian ships = Small Cargo, Large Cargo, Colony Ship, Recycler and Espionage Probe, not Pathfinder. Probe cargo depends on universe settings.

Stacking, caps and special cases — Read before combining percentages or comparing a displayed final bonus.

Source vintage and current-game limits

All rows were checked against Gameforge’s “PTS LF values (current)” workbook sheet, file modified 4 December 2023, and OGame Tracker commit 052f9dd83ceb18e3188706be4688f0239b8a9c21. Review date: 9 November 2026. This is a source-based reference, not a new live-account measurement.

Caps belong to effect categories rather than independently to each row. Where no cap is specified in the definitions, do not infer that every final game system is unlimited. Later official changes and current Techinfo take precedence. Known fuel-cap, discovery-speed and crawler-output uncertainties are explained in the shared reference.

Quick checks:
- The technology’s target matches the benefit being compared.
- Base coefficients have not been mistaken for the final account bonus.
- Current Techinfo and the shared caps guidance have been checked.
