Mars server / 2026-10-27

US Mars server settings

Published settings

The official US announcement identifies Mars as server 268 with ×10 economy, ×20 research and ×1 fleet speeds. The settings listed in our Mars reference match the owner’s 11 November panel.

For Mars: Use the Mars reference for local calculation inputs. The announcement does not establish Orion activation on Mars.

Changelog / 2026-11-05

Lifeform discovery-limit changes

Announced · deployment unconfirmed

Gameforge plans to adjust blocked discovery counters and remove the limit on servers older than one year. The limit remains on servers up to one year old.

For Mars: An older transferred account is not the same as an older server. The announcement gives a November window; activation on Mars has not been confirmed here.

Changelog / 2026-11-05

Version 13.1.0 and r5 hotfix

Official release notices

The live-version notice schedules 13.1.0 for 5 November and the r5 hotfix for 6 November. Orion’s initial activation is explicitly limited to new universes opening on 6 November.

For Mars: These are general live-server notices. They do not prove that Orion is enabled on Mars; check its availability in game.
