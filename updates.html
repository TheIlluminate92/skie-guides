Mars server / 2026-10-27

US Mars server settings

Published settings

The official US announcement identifies Mars as server 268 with ×10 economy, ×20 research and ×1 fleet speeds. The settings listed in our Mars reference match the owner’s 11 November panel.

For Mars: Use the Mars reference for local calculation inputs. The announcement does not establish Orion activation on Mars.

Changelog / 2026-11-30

Orion opens on existing universes

Scheduled · around 10:00 CET

Gameforge scheduled global activation for Monday, 30 November, around 04:00 on the US Mars server clock or 03:00 US Central. New universes that opened on 6 November received Orion first.

For Mars: Mars reported +30 bonus fields on 26 November. Setting the bonus to +70 would add 40 fields per planet. Check the setting and scanner after activation.

Changelog / 2026-11-26

Version 13.2.0-r3 reaches Mars

Version verified in server data

Light Fighter missions award ships at anomaly levels 250, 600 and 900. Three additional enemy fleet compositions start at 250. Buildings, research and Lifeform research gain a +5 button. Both +5 and +10 combine levels in one queue entry with combined costs and time reductions.

For Mars: Ordinary ACS attacks and defenses allow six players. Combat reports show the correct ships when switching participants or rounds, and Orion invitations count members at the correct time.

Changelog / 2026-11-26

Items, auctions and alliance fixes

13.2.0-r3 release notes

Class changes consume time-limited items first. A stronger expedition resource booster can replace a weaker one. Technologies raised with items count immediately toward highscore achievements. Auctioneer refreshes show the item quantity correctly.

For Mars: Broken alliance images no longer block the alliance page. An unnamed alliance rank no longer leaves the page loading indefinitely.

Live changelog

Changelog / 2026-11-17

Lifeform discovery limits and scanner display

Live changelog

The age-based discovery limit was removed on rounds older than one year. The update fixed the high-level scanner cost display, free mobile relocations checking the Dark Matter balance, new mobile accounts appearing inactive, and misaligned anomaly messages.

For Mars: The age rule follows the destination universe. Mars opened in November 2026, so transferred accounts here still play on a young round.

Changelog / 2026-11-05

Lifeform discovery-limit changes

Original announcement · see 17 November

Gameforge announced a one-time correction for counters carried above a younger destination’s limit. Discovery history is retained.

For Mars: The 17 November notes confirm the age-rule change. Check the in-game counter for the outcome on an individual transferred account.

Changelog / 2026-11-05

Version 13.1.0 and r5 hotfix

Official release notices

The live-version notice schedules 13.1.0 for 5 November and the r5 hotfix for 6 November. Orion’s initial activation is explicitly limited to new universes opening on 6 November.

For Mars: These are general live-server notices. They do not prove that Orion is enabled on Mars; check its availability in game.
