SPECIAL BUILDINGS / A PRACTICAL REFERENCE

Unlock a capability.
Know its limits.

A moon is a platform, not a safety guarantee. A gate moves ships, not your resources. Nanites save construction time, not the cost of the queue. Orion scanners open mission progression when the feature is enabled. Follow these guides to build the tool you need and use it correctly.
