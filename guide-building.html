GUIDE 01 / Beginner

Choose the next useful investment.

Compare additional net income, the full support cost and the resource shortage blocking the next goal.

3 MIN READ8 SECTIONSREVIEWED 2026-11-09

Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026. Read the linked reference for formulas, sources and version limits; current account settings remain the final check.

At a glance

Compare the benefit of the next level with its full cost and the time you can use it. Fix the limiting resource or prerequisite before buying a bigger queue.

SECTION 01

Know what each mine does

Mines provide recurring Metal, Crystal and Deuterium while power and storage permit. Compare the next level’s additional output, not the planet’s total income. Temperature, position and active bonuses can change the useful choice.

Details and worked examples →
SECTION 02

Early production and infrastructure

Early growth needs both production and the facilities for a useful unlock. Fund the missing resource and its supporting power rather than copying a fixed mine ratio. The detailed reference keeps the first-planet checklist.

Details and worked examples →
SECTION 03

Measure before you spend

Read current production, power, storage and fields on each candidate planet. Keep the same class, universe speed and active bonuses in both sides of a comparison. A shortage of power or collection capacity is not solved by another mine.

Details and worked examples →
SECTION 04

Name the next constraint

The limiting resource is the one that delays the intended purchase longest. Use current stock and reliable net production, then include deliveries at their actual arrival times. Reserve flight fuel and check infrastructure before spending.

Details and worked examples →
SECTION 05

Compare the complete investment

Simple payback divides the complete project cost by its extra net income. Include supporting power and operating fuel, use consistent resource values, and compare across planets. A shorter payback can still lose to an urgent unlock or resource constraint.

Details and worked examples →
SECTION 06

Make the offline interval affordable

Current stock plus production during your absence is an unconstrained forecast. A full store stops further mine accumulation. Price storage against production actually saved, and include known deliveries and spending separately. More storage does not make a stockpile safe.

Details and worked examples →
SECTION 07

Verify the result

After completion, compare actual output under the same settings. Check energy, storage, production sliders and bonus expiry if the gain differs. Keep one funded improvement, one next target and enough reserves to protect the operation.
